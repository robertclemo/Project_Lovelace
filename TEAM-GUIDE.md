# Lovelace — What It Is and How to Use It

*A plain-English guide. No tech background needed.*

## What is this?

Lovelace is a phone app being built for our Resistance cell. It puts two
things you already use in one place:

1. **The intel map** — the same enhanced Ingress map view people
   normally get through a browser add-on (IITC), but built right into
   the app.
2. **Team chat** — a private chat just for our cell, separate from
   texting or Discord.

It's still being built, so think of it as an early test version, not a
finished product. Some things work great already; other things
(described below) aren't built yet.

## Important things to know before you use it

- **This is a work-in-progress test version.** Things may break, change,
  or need to be reinstalled as it's improved.
- **Chat is not private/encrypted yet.** For now, treat team chat in
  this app like a regular group text — fine for coordination, but don't
  say anything in it you wouldn't want others to potentially see.
  End-to-end encryption (so only teammates can read messages) is planned
  but not built yet.
- **This app never touches your actual Ingress gameplay.** It just shows
  you the same map info a browser would. It doesn't play the game for
  you or automate anything.

## Getting the app on your phone

Since this isn't on the Google Play Store (it's a private test build),
you'll get a download link directly from Robert. Installing it involves
a couple of extra taps compared to a normal app store install:

1. Open the link Robert sends you on your phone.
2. Download the file.
3. When you open it, your phone will probably show a warning like "Install
   unknown app" or "blocked by Play Protect" — this is normal for a test
   app that isn't on the Play Store. Tap through to allow it (usually a
   button that says "Install anyway" or "Settings" → toggle "Allow from
   this source").
4. Once installed, open it like any other app.

## Signing in (there are two separate logins — this is normal)

**Step 1 — Sign into the app itself.**
When you first open Lovelace, tap **"Sign in with Google."** This just
lets the app know who you are for team chat — pick whichever Google
account you want to use for that.

**Step 2 — Sign into Ingress, inside the map.**
Tap over to the **Map** tab. You'll see a screen that looks like the
regular Ingress intel sign-in page. Sign in here the exact same way you
normally would for Ingress (Google or Apple, whichever you use for the
game). This step is what actually lets you see the live map with
portals, links, and fields.

These two sign-ins can be the same account or different ones — the app
keeps them separate on purpose, so your actual Ingress/Niantic login is
never touched or stored by the app itself.

## Using the map

Once signed in, the Map tab shows the live intel map — portals, links,
fields, and colors, same as you'd see through a browser. You can pan,
zoom, and use the same map controls (+ / − buttons, layers icon in the
corner) you're used to.

## Using chat

Tap the **Chat** tab. Type a message in the box at the bottom and tap
the send arrow. Messages from you show up in a different color than
messages from teammates, so it's easy to follow who said what.
Everyone on the team sees the same chat in real time.

## If something looks broken

This is genuinely an early test build, so:
- If the map or chat shows a blank white screen, try closing the app
  fully and reopening it.
- If sign-in seems stuck, the same fix usually works: fully close and
  reopen the app.
- Otherwise, just tell Robert what you saw (a screenshot helps a lot)
  and he'll sort it out.

## What's coming later

- Private, encrypted chat (so messages can't be read by anyone but the
  team).
- A way to invite new members with a code or QR scan.
- Live "who's looking at the map right now" and a way for one person to
  lead everyone else's view during an op.
- Push notifications, an SOS button, shared pins for things like farm
  routes or safe hack spots, and a few other coordination tools.

None of that is in this test build yet — what's described above (sign
in, map, chat) is everything that currently works.
